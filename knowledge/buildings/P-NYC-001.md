## Pets
<!-- topics: pets -->
Cats and dogs are allowed, up to 2 pets per apartment. Register each pet with the office before it moves in. Pets must be registered with resident services before move-in; a dog run is on site. Assistance animals are not pets and are handled through a reasonable accommodation request. There is no separate pet charge in the fee schedule.

## Parking and bikes
<!-- topics: parking -->
Covered garages (6, operated by LAZ Parking); there is a waitlist (24/7 garages with valet and park & lock options). Parking is optional and priced in the fee schedule.

## Packages and deliveries
<!-- topics: amenities -->
Packages go to the Smart Package Room, open 24/7; you get a pickup code by text or email.

## Moving in and out
<!-- topics: moving -->
Moves are scheduled with resident services at least 5 business days ahead, Monday to Saturday 8 AM - 6 PM. Movers must provide a certificate of insurance naming the owner and operator.

## Leases and renewals
<!-- topics: leasing, rent_and_fees -->
Leases are 12 or 24 months. Renewal offers go out about 90 days before the lease ends; rent stabilized apartments receive renewals on the state form within the required window. Application, deposit and monthly charges are listed in the fee schedule.
