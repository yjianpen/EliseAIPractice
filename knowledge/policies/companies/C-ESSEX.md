# Essex Property Trust - Resident Handbook (practice)

> SYNTHETIC PRACTICE DOCUMENT. Essex Property Trust is a real company, but this guidebook was written for interview practice. It is not Essex Property Trust's actual resident policy; service standards, credits and procedures are invented, and phone numbers are fictional.

Practice handbook for Essex buildings in San Francisco. Your property addendum overrides this
handbook where they differ.

## Submitting a service request
<!-- topics: repairs_timeline, plumbing, paint_plaster, doors_windows, appliances, flooring_stairs, intercom | cities: all -->
All service requests go through the resident app; requests by email are entered by staff within one
business day. Choose a category, describe the problem, and add photos. You can give permission to enter when you
submit, which lets us schedule faster. You will see status changes in the app: received, scheduled, technician
assigned, completed.

## Emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, water_supply, heat, hot_water, electrical, elevator, smoke_co_alarms | cities: all -->
Call the Essex emergency line for gas odors, flooding, sewage, no water, no hot water, no heat where Essex
controls the heat, electrical sparks or burning smells, or an elevator entrapment. For a gas odor, leave first and
call 911 and PG&E from outside. In San Francisco, also be ready for earthquakes: know where your gas shut-off is but
do not turn gas back on yourself after it has been shut off.

## Service timelines
<!-- topics: repairs_timeline, heat, hot_water, gas, water_leak, pests, mold | cities: all -->
Essex service standards (company policy):
- Emergency: on-call technician dispatched within 2 hours, any time.
- Priority (pests, mold, leaks, appliance failures, smoke/CO alarm issues): visit within 3 business days.
- Standard: visit within 10 business days.
Overdue requests are flagged to the community manager automatically. State law separately gives tenants remedies if
serious habitability repairs are not made within a reasonable time.

## Heat and hot water
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water | cities: San Francisco -->
Where Essex supplies the heat, every habitable room must be able to reach 70F at any hour. If your unit has its own
heater, check the thermostat and the breaker first, then submit a priority request if it still does not heat. No
hot water is an emergency.

## Entry to your home
<!-- topics: entry_access | cities: San Francisco -->
We give written notice at least 24 hours before entering (6 days if mailed), with the date, approximate time and
purpose, and we enter during business hours: 8 AM to 6 PM, Monday to Friday. Exceptions are emergencies, or when you
are home and agree at the time. If you pre-authorize entry in the app, the app notice counts as your written notice.

## Pest control
<!-- topics: pests, bedbugs, garbage | cities: all -->
Essex contracts a licensed pest control operator who services common areas monthly. Request in-unit service in the
app. For suspected bed bugs, submit a request right away; we inspect within 3 business days and treat at no cost to
you. Every new resident receives our bed bug information notice with the lease.

## Mold and moisture
<!-- topics: mold, water_leak | cities: all -->
Report visible mold and water intrusion as priority requests. We find and fix the moisture source, then clean or
remediate the mold. Run bathroom and kitchen fans and open windows when weather allows.

## Lead-based paint
<!-- topics: lead, paint_plaster | cities: San Francisco -->
Homes built before 1978 come with the federal lead disclosure and EPA pamphlet. Report peeling paint promptly. Paint
repairs in pre-1978 homes are done by lead-safe certified contractors.

## Neighbors and noise
<!-- topics: noise | cities: all -->
Quiet hours are 10 PM to 7 AM unless your property addendum says otherwise. Use the "Neighbor concern" category in
the app with dates and times. We contact the other resident and escalate under the lease if it continues. For
disturbances in progress, call SFPD non-emergency or 911.

## Trash, recycling and compost
<!-- topics: garbage | cities: all -->
San Francisco requires sorting into three Recology bins: black for trash, blue for recycling, green for compost.
Keep lids closed. Bulky item collection is scheduled through the community manager.

## Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
<!-- topics: smoke_co_alarms | cities: all -->
Essex installs and annually checks smoke and CO alarms. Residents test them monthly and report problems as
priority requests. Do not remove batteries.

## Retaliation and your rights
<!-- topics: retaliation | cities: all -->
Essex does not retaliate against residents for repair requests, complaints to the city, or tenant organizing.
Concerns go to the regional director at the help email.
