# Lumen Park Residential - Resident Guidebook

Synthetic guidebook for practice. It applies to every Lumen Park building. Sections marked for one city apply only
there. A property addendum for your building overrides this guidebook where they differ.

## How to request maintenance
<!-- topics: repairs_timeline, plumbing, paint_plaster, doors_windows, appliances, flooring_stairs, intercom | cities: all -->
Submit every repair request through the resident portal or by text to the maintenance line so it gets a work-order
number. Include the room, what you see, when it started, and photos if you can. A phone call or a conversation with
staff is fine for emergencies, but we still create a work order so there is a record. You will get updates by your
preferred channel when the request is scheduled, when a technician is on the way, and when it is closed.

## Emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, water_supply, heat, hot_water, electrical, elevator, smoke_co_alarms | cities: all -->
Call the 24-hour emergency line right away for: a gas smell, active flooding or a ceiling leak, sewage backup, no
water, no heat or no hot water, a full power outage in your apartment, a carbon monoxide alarm, or someone trapped in
an elevator. For a gas smell or a CO alarm, leave the apartment first and call 911 from outside. For fire or anyone
in danger, call 911 first. The emergency line is staffed 24/7 and dispatches an on-call technician.

## Response time standards
<!-- topics: repairs_timeline, heat, hot_water, gas, water_leak, pests, mold | cities: all -->
Our service standards (company policy, separate from legal deadlines):
- Emergency: technician contact within 1 hour, on site within 4 hours, and the hazard made safe the same day.
- Urgent (pests, active slow leaks, broken locks, elevator out, smoke/CO alarm problems): scheduled within 2 business days.
- Routine (paint, cabinets, doors, appliances that still partly work): scheduled within 7 business days.
If a request misses these targets, reply to the work-order message and it is escalated to your property manager.
You always keep the right to contact the city about conditions in your apartment.

## Service outage credits
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water, water_supply, elevator, repairs_timeline | cities: all -->
If building heat, hot water or water service is out for more than 24 consecutive hours because of a building-system
failure, affected residents receive a prorated rent credit for each full day of the outage. Elevator outages over
72 hours in elevator buildings also qualify. Credits are applied automatically to the next month's ledger.

## Heat and hot water - New York City
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water | cities: New York -->
Heat season runs October 1 through May 31. Where the building supplies heat we keep apartments at least 68F between
6 AM and 10 PM whenever it is below 55F outside, and at least 62F between 10 PM and 6 AM. Hot water at the tap is at
least 120F all year. If you are below these levels, report it as an emergency. Helpful details: the room, the
thermometer reading, the time, and whether radiators are cold or just warm.

## Heat and hot water - San Francisco
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water | cities: San Francisco -->
Where Lumen controls the heat, every habitable room must be able to reach 70F at any time of day. Where you control
your own heater, report equipment that does not heat as an urgent repair. Hot and cold running water must work at
all fixtures; report no hot water as an emergency.

## Entering your apartment - New York City
<!-- topics: entry_access, repairs_timeline | cities: New York -->
For non-emergency repairs and inspections we give advance notice and schedule a window with you; our standard is at
least 24 hours' notice for repairs and one week for non-urgent improvements or inspections. In an emergency, such as
water coming through a ceiling or a gas smell, staff may enter without advance notice to prevent injury or damage,
and we will leave a written note. Please give reasonable access for needed repairs.

## Entering your apartment - San Francisco
<!-- topics: entry_access, repairs_timeline | cities: San Francisco -->
Except in emergencies or when you agree at the time, we give written notice at least 24 hours before entry (6 days
if mailed) stating the date, approximate time and purpose, and we enter during normal business hours. If you agree
to a repair visit by phone or text, we can confirm a time within one week without a separate written notice.

## Pests and extermination
<!-- topics: pests, garbage | cities: all -->
We use integrated pest management: sealing holes and gaps, fixing leaks that attract pests, cleaning, and limited,
targeted treatment by licensed exterminators. Monthly building-wide extermination is scheduled through the portal;
you can also request a visit for your apartment at any time. Please store food in sealed containers and bag
garbage. Rodents and roaches are treated as urgent.

## Pests - New York City annual inspections
<!-- topics: pests, mold | cities: New York -->
Under NYC's indoor allergen law we inspect every apartment once a year for pests and mold, clean and check vacant
apartments before move-in, and include the indoor allergen notice with each lease.

## Bed bugs
<!-- topics: bedbugs, pests | cities: all -->
Report suspected bed bugs in writing through the portal as soon as you notice them. Do not throw away furniture or
treat it yourself; a licensed exterminator inspects your apartment and adjacent units and gives you a preparation
checklist. Treatment is at no cost to you.

## Mold and moisture
<!-- topics: mold, water_leak | cities: all -->
Report mold, musty odors and water stains right away; mold usually means a leak or ventilation problem that we must
fix first. Small areas are cleaned by our staff using safe work practices. Larger areas are handled by licensed
mold professionals. Keep bathroom fans running during showers and leave a gap behind furniture on exterior walls.

## Mold - New York City size thresholds
<!-- topics: mold | cities: New York -->
In buildings with 10 or more apartments, mold over 10 square feet in an apartment is assessed and removed by
separate, licensed mold assessment and remediation contractors.

## Lead paint - New York City
<!-- topics: lead, paint_plaster | cities: New York -->
In buildings covered by NYC's lead law we send an annual notice in January asking whether a child under 6 lives in
your apartment or spends 10 or more hours a week there. Please return it, and tell us any time a child under 6
moves in. Report peeling or chipping paint immediately. Lead work is done by certified workers with dust controls.

## Lead paint - San Francisco
<!-- topics: lead, paint_plaster | cities: San Francisco -->
For buildings built before 1978 we give the federal lead disclosure form and the EPA lead pamphlet before you sign
your lease. Report peeling or chipping paint right away and do not sand or scrape it yourself.

## Noise and neighbors
<!-- topics: noise | cities: all -->
Default quiet hours are 10 PM to 8 AM unless your property addendum says otherwise. For repeated noise from another
apartment, send the dates, times and a description through the portal. We contact the other household and, if it
continues, enforce the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, call the police non-emergency line
or 911. Construction or renovation work by Lumen happens only on weekdays from 8 AM to 5 PM unless it is an emergency.

## Garbage, recycling and bulk items
<!-- topics: garbage, pests | cities: all -->
Follow your building's collection schedule in the property addendum. Break down boxes, rinse recyclables, and never
leave bags in hallways or stairwells. Request bulk-item pickup through the portal at least 3 days ahead.

## Smoke and carbon monoxide alarms
<!-- topics: smoke_co_alarms | cities: all -->
Every apartment has working smoke and CO alarms at move-in. Test them monthly. Report a missing, damaged or
constantly chirping alarm as urgent. Never disable an alarm. If a CO alarm sounds, leave and call 911.

## Window guards - New York City
<!-- topics: window_guards | cities: New York -->
If a child 10 or younger lives in your apartment, or you ask for them, we install approved window guards on all
windows except a fire-escape window, at no cost. Return the annual window guard notice each January.

## Snow and ice
<!-- topics: snow_ice | cities: New York -->
Building staff clear and salt sidewalks and entrances after snowfall. Report icy walkways or entrances to the
maintenance line.

## No retaliation
<!-- topics: retaliation, repairs_timeline | cities: all -->
Lumen will never penalize you for requesting repairs, reporting conditions to the city, or joining a tenant
association. If you believe a staff member has treated you unfairly after a complaint, contact the regional manager
at the office email.
