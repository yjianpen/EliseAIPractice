# Greenpoint Walk-up - Property Addendum

This addendum applies only to Greenpoint Walk-up (118 Oakfield St, Brooklyn, NY 11222). Where it differs from the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) Resident Guidebook, this addendum controls.

## Building contacts and emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, electrical, elevator, heat, repairs_timeline -->
On-site contact: Marek Nowicki, 718-555-0111. Office hours: Mon-Fri 9 AM - 5 PM. After hours, call the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) emergency line at 212-555-0100. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 from outside, then the gas utility.

## Heat and hot water at this building
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water -->
Heat comes from a central steam boiler (oil-to-gas conversion 2019) that management controls; from October 1 to May 31 the building keeps apartments at least 68F from 6 AM to 10 PM when it is below 55F outside, and at least 62F overnight. Hot water is supplied by a boiler-fired indirect tank. If your apartment is below these levels, report it right away as an emergency work order and note the room temperature and time.

## Quiet hours and noise
<!-- topics: noise -->
Quiet hours at Greenpoint Walk-up are 10:00 PM - 8:00 AM. Report repeated noise from another apartment through the portal with dates and times; management follows up with the other household under the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, contact local police.

## Trash, recycling and compost
<!-- topics: garbage, pests -->
Trash: Mon, Thu (bags out after 8 PM the night before). Recycling: Thu. Bag all food waste; loose garbage in common areas attracts pests.

## Laundry
<!-- topics: appliances -->
None on site; laundromat at corner.

## Pets and smoking
<!-- topics: noise -->
Pets: cats allowed; dogs under 25 lb with addendum. Smoking: no smoking in common areas.

## Lead paint (building built before 1960)
<!-- topics: lead, paint_plaster -->
Greenpoint Walk-up was built in 1928, so NYC Local Law 1 applies. Each January we send a notice asking whether a child under 6 lives in or regularly spends 10+ hours a week in your apartment; please return it. Report peeling or chipping paint immediately. Lead work is done only by certified workers using safe work practices.

## Window guards
<!-- topics: window_guards -->
If a child 10 or under lives in your apartment, or you simply want them, tell the office and we install approved window guards at no charge. Do not remove guards yourself.

## Rent regulation
<!-- topics: retaliation, repairs_timeline -->
Most apartments here are rent stabilized. Rent increases on renewal follow the NYC Rent Guidelines Board orders. Questions about your rent history go to the office in writing.
