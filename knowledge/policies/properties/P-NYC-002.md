# Astoria Gardens - Property Addendum

This addendum applies only to Astoria Gardens (31-40 Linden Ave, Astoria, NY 11106). Where it differs from the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) Resident Guidebook, this addendum controls.

## Building contacts and emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, electrical, elevator, heat, repairs_timeline -->
On-site contact: Luis Ortega, 718-555-0122. Office hours: Mon-Sat 8 AM - 6 PM. After hours, call the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) emergency line at 212-555-0100. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 from outside, then the gas utility.

## Heat and hot water at this building
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water -->
Heat comes from a central hot-water boiler with apartment thermostats that management controls; from October 1 to May 31 the building keeps apartments at least 68F from 6 AM to 10 PM when it is below 55F outside, and at least 62F overnight. Hot water is supplied by a central gas water heaters. If your apartment is below these levels, report it right away as an emergency work order and note the room temperature and time.

## Quiet hours and noise
<!-- topics: noise -->
Quiet hours at Astoria Gardens are 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM. Report repeated noise from another apartment through the portal with dates and times; management follows up with the other household under the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, contact local police.

## Trash, recycling and compost
<!-- topics: garbage, pests -->
Trash: Tue, Fri (compactor chute 8 AM - 9 PM). Recycling: Fri. Bag all food waste; loose garbage in common areas attracts pests.

## Laundry
<!-- topics: appliances -->
Basement laundry room 7 am - 10 pm.

## Pets and smoking
<!-- topics: noise -->
Pets: dogs and cats allowed with addendum, max 2. Smoking: smoke-free building.

## Elevator
<!-- topics: elevator -->
If the elevator is out of service, report it immediately. Residents with mobility needs can ask the office for assistance with groceries and packages until service is restored. If someone is trapped, call 911 first.

## Window guards
<!-- topics: window_guards -->
If a child 10 or under lives in your apartment, or you simply want them, tell the office and we install approved window guards at no charge. Do not remove guards yourself.
