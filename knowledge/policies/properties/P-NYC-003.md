# Hudson Yards Tower - Property Addendum

This addendum applies only to Hudson Yards Tower (455 W 35th St, New York, NY 10001). Where it differs from the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) Resident Guidebook, this addendum controls.

## Building contacts and emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, electrical, elevator, heat, repairs_timeline -->
On-site contact: Priya Raman (resident manager), 212-555-0133. Office hours: daily 8 AM - 8 PM. After hours, call the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) emergency line at 212-555-0100. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 from outside, then the gas utility.

## Heat and hot water at this building
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water -->
Your apartment has in-unit heat pumps (PTAC), tenant thermostat. You control the thermostat, so first check that it is on, set above room temperature, and that the breaker has not tripped. If the equipment itself does not produce heat, submit an urgent work order. Hot water comes from central high-efficiency boilers.

## Quiet hours and noise
<!-- topics: noise -->
Quiet hours at Hudson Yards Tower are 11:00 PM - 7:00 AM. Report repeated noise from another apartment through the portal with dates and times; management follows up with the other household under the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, contact local police.

## Trash, recycling and compost
<!-- topics: garbage, pests -->
Trash: daily chute; bulk items via concierge. Recycling: daily chute. Bag all food waste; loose garbage in common areas attracts pests.

## Laundry
<!-- topics: appliances -->
In-unit washer/dryer.

## Pets and smoking
<!-- topics: noise -->
Pets: dogs and cats allowed, pet spa on 3rd floor. Smoking: smoke-free building.

## Elevator
<!-- topics: elevator -->
If the elevator is out of service, report it immediately. Residents with mobility needs can ask the office for assistance with groceries and packages until service is restored. If someone is trapped, call 911 first.

## Window guards
<!-- topics: window_guards -->
If a child 10 or under lives in your apartment, or you simply want them, tell the office and we install approved window guards at no charge. Do not remove guards yourself.
