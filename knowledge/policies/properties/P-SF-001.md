# Mission Dolores Flats - Property Addendum

This addendum applies only to Mission Dolores Flats (2140 Valencia Ct, San Francisco, CA 94110). Where it differs from the Bayline Homes (synthetic) Resident Guidebook, this addendum controls.

## Building contacts and emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, electrical, elevator, heat, repairs_timeline -->
On-site contact: Dana Whitfield (on-site manager), 415-555-0144. Office hours: Mon-Fri 10 AM - 4 PM. After hours, call the Bayline Homes (synthetic) emergency line at 415-555-0100. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 from outside, then the gas utility.

## Heat and hot water at this building
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water -->
Heat comes from a central gas furnace, landlord-controlled that management controls; the building heating system keeps every habitable room able to reach 70F at all times. Hot water is supplied by a shared gas water heater. If your apartment is below these levels, report it right away as an emergency work order and note the room temperature and time.

## Quiet hours and noise
<!-- topics: noise -->
Quiet hours at Mission Dolores Flats are 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM. Report repeated noise from another apartment through the portal with dates and times; management follows up with the other household under the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, contact local police.

## Trash, recycling and compost

## Lead paint disclosure (built before 1978)
<!-- topics: lead, paint_plaster -->
Mission Dolores Flats was built in 1925. Your lease package includes the federal lead disclosure form and the EPA lead pamphlet. Report peeling or chipping paint right away; do not sand or scrape it yourself.

## Rent regulation
<!-- topics: retaliation, repairs_timeline -->
This building is covered by the San Francisco Rent Ordinance. Allowable annual increases are set by the SF Rent Board. Questions about your rent go to the office in writing.
