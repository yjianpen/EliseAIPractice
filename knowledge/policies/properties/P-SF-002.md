# SoMa Commons - Property Addendum

This addendum applies only to SoMa Commons (88 Harriet Way, San Francisco, CA 94103). Where it differs from the Bayline Homes (synthetic) Resident Guidebook, this addendum controls.

## Building contacts and emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, electrical, elevator, heat, repairs_timeline -->
On-site contact: Jordan Lee (community manager), 415-555-0155. Office hours: daily 9 AM - 6 PM. After hours, call the Bayline Homes (synthetic) emergency line at 415-555-0100. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 from outside, then the gas utility.

## Heat and hot water at this building
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water -->
Your apartment has in-unit electric wall heaters, tenant thermostat. You control the thermostat, so first check that it is on, set above room temperature, and that the breaker has not tripped. If the equipment itself does not produce heat, submit an urgent work order. Hot water comes from central boilers.

## Quiet hours and noise
<!-- topics: noise -->
Quiet hours at SoMa Commons are 10:00 PM - 8:00 AM. Report repeated noise from another apartment through the portal with dates and times; management follows up with the other household under the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, contact local police.

## Trash, recycling and compost
<!-- topics: garbage, pests -->
Trash: trash room daily; Recology pickup Mon/Thu. Recycling: Mon/Thu. Bag all food waste; loose garbage in common areas attracts pests.

## Laundry
<!-- topics: appliances -->
In-unit washer/dryer.

## Pets and smoking
<!-- topics: noise -->
Pets: dogs and cats allowed with addendum. Smoking: smoke-free building.

## Elevator
<!-- topics: elevator -->
If the elevator is out of service, report it immediately. Residents with mobility needs can ask the office for assistance with groceries and packages until service is restored. If someone is trapped, call 911 first.
