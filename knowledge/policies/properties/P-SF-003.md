# Sunset Terrace - Property Addendum

This addendum applies only to Sunset Terrace (1530 Noriega Pl, San Francisco, CA 94122). Where it differs from the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) Resident Guidebook, this addendum controls.

## Building contacts and emergencies
<!-- topics: gas, water_leak, electrical, elevator, heat, repairs_timeline -->
On-site contact: Lumen SF maintenance desk, 415-555-0166. Office hours: Mon-Fri 9 AM - 5 PM. After hours, call the Lumen Park Residential (synthetic) emergency line at 212-555-0100. If you smell gas, leave the building first and call 911 from outside, then the gas utility.

## Heat and hot water at this building
<!-- topics: heat, hot_water -->
Your apartment has individual gas wall furnaces, tenant-controlled. You control the thermostat, so first check that it is on, set above room temperature, and that the breaker has not tripped. If the equipment itself does not produce heat, submit an urgent work order. Hot water comes from individual tank water heaters.

## Quiet hours and noise
<!-- topics: noise -->
Quiet hours at Sunset Terrace are 10:00 PM - 7:00 AM. Report repeated noise from another apartment through the portal with dates and times; management follows up with the other household under the lease. For noise happening right now that feels unsafe, contact local police.

## Trash, recycling and compost
<!-- topics: garbage, pests -->
Trash: Tue (Recology). Recycling: Tue. Bag all food waste; loose garbage in common areas attracts pests.

## Pets and smoking
<!-- topics: noise -->
Pets: no pets except service/assistance animals. Smoking: no smoking anywhere on property.

## Lead paint disclosure (built before 1978)
<!-- topics: lead, paint_plaster -->
Sunset Terrace was built in 1952. Your lease package includes the federal lead disclosure form and the EPA lead pamphlet. Report peeling or chipping paint right away; do not sand or scrape it yourself.

## Rent regulation
<!-- topics: retaliation, repairs_timeline -->
This building is covered by the San Francisco Rent Ordinance. Allowable annual increases are set by the SF Rent Board. Questions about your rent go to the office in writing.
